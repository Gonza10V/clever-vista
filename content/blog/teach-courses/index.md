Use the developed infrastructure to design genetic networks for biocomputing using genetic logic circuits, in-vivo AI, and other unconventional computing such as phase-based genetic logic circuits and reservoir computing. We will implement these genetic circuits to create organisms and communities with new sensing, computing, production, and reporting capabilities.
